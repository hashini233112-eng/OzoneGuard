    About OzoneGuard

    OzoneGuard is a smart classroom cooling optimization concept designed to encourage energy-efficient and responsible cooling practices in educational environments.

    Classrooms can experience different temperature and occupancy conditions throughout the day. However, cooling systems may continue operating at the same setting even when classroom conditions change. This can lead to unnecessary energy use.

    Our Objective

    The main objective of OzoneGuard is to provide simple, understandable cooling recommendations based on classroom temperature, student count, occupancy level, and the current AC setting.

    How OzoneGuard Helps

    • Encourages moderate AC temperature settings.
    • Considers classroom occupancy before recommending cooling.
    • Encourages the use of fans for better air circulation.
    • Suggests natural ventilation when conditions are suitable.
    • Helps users become more aware of unnecessary cooling.

    Current Prototype

    The current prototype is developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and rule-based logic. It does not require sensors or a backend system. Users manually enter classroom conditions and receive an immediate recommendation.

    Future Scope

    In the future, OzoneGuard can be expanded with IoT sensors, real-time temperature monitoring, occupancy detection, weather information, smart AC integration, machine learning, and energy-consumption tracking.

    Our Vision

    Our vision is to create smarter classrooms where cooling systems are used efficiently without compromising classroom comfort. By combining technology with sustainable practices, OzoneGuard aims to support energy-conscious educational spaces.
