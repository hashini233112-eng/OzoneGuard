    About OzoneGuard

    OzoneGuard is a software-based prototype designed to encourage energy-efficient cooling practices in classrooms.

    The system considers temperature and occupancy conditions and provides simple recommendations for responsible AC usage.

    Future development can include sensors, weather data, smart AC integration and machine learning-based energy prediction.
